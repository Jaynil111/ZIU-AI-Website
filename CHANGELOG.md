# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — November 2026

First complete version of the ZIU AI website.

- 25 pages built from *ZIU AI Website Content v1.0*, with placeholders highlighted where content is still to come.
- Ray-traced vertical glass wall: live on the homepage, sharp stills on page headers, call-to-action bands and the founder portrait.
- Cinematic homepage intro (about 2.5 seconds) with Skip and replay from the logo.
- Contact form and automation check connected to Web3Forms, with spam trap and retry on failure.
- Savings calculator on Resources that can prefill the contact form.
- Full-screen "ZIU AI" finale lit by the cursor; glass cursor on desktop.
- Smooth scrolling, page transitions and scroll animations, all off under reduced motion.
- Site checks: approved copy per page, links, headings, titles and form labels (`npm run check`).
